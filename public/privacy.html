Last updated: June 4, 2026

Overview

Sitecraft Digital Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you provide when you use our website or engage our services.

Information We Collect

When you fill out a contact form or inquiry on our website, we collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, business name, email address, and phone number.

How We Use Your Information

Any information you provide to us is used solely for the purpose of executing the creation and management of your website. We use your contact details to respond to your inquiry, discuss your project, and deliver our services to you.

We Do Not Sell or Share Your Information

Your personal information will never be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Period. The only reason we collect your information is to serve you as a client.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. Your data is only accessible to Sitecraft Digital Inc. team members who need it to provide services to you.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use basic third-party tools (such as form submission services) solely to facilitate communication with us. These services do not receive your data for their own use.
