Why Sitecraft Digital Inc. Exists

My name is Daniel Mercier, CEO of Sitecraft Digital Inc. I have an extensive background in marketing and AI integration. I noticed that a lot of local companies didn't have a website and had very little to no online presence when talking to some of these business owners.

The main reason was because most web designers were charging very high upfront costs and unaffordable monthly fees to manage their website. With such a big problem, I had to take action and founded Sitecraft Digital Inc. to help small business owners compete in local markets.
